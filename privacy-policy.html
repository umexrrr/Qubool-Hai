Privacy Policy

At Qubool Hai Events & Decor, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, enquiry form, WhatsApp, phone, Instagram, or other communication channels, we may receive information such as your name, phone number, event details, event date, venue/location, guest count, and any message or enquiry you voluntarily provide.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Discuss your event requirements
  • Provide information about our services
  • Coordinate event planning and decoration requirements
  • Communicate with you regarding your enquiry

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may only be shared when necessary to provide requested services, comply with applicable law, or protect our legal rights.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through our website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party services such as WhatsApp and Instagram. Their use is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Your Choices

You may contact us if you want to ask about the personal information you have provided to us or request that we stop using it for communication related to your enquiry, subject to applicable legal requirements.
