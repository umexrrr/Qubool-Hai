Creating Beautiful Beginnings

“Qubool Hai Events & Decor brings together thoughtful design, floral artistry, elegant backdrops and atmospheric lighting to create celebrations that feel personal, beautiful and unforgettable.”

Based in the historic city of Indore, we specialize in transforming sacred Nikah ceremonies and wedding venues into serene, romantic sanctuaries. Guided by subtle textures, soft baby pink tones, and pure white florals, every arrangement is curated with devotion to authenticity and contemporary grandeur.
