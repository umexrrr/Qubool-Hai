Creating Beautiful Beginnings

“Qubool Hai Events & Decor is an Indore-based wedding and event décor studio specializing in elegant Muslim wedding and Nikah celebrations. From bespoke Nikah stages and floral arrangements to custom backdrops, lighting and complete event styling, we create thoughtfully designed celebrations tailored to each occasion.”

Based in the historic city of Indore, we specialize in transforming sacred Nikah ceremonies and wedding venues into serene, romantic sanctuaries. Guided by subtle textures, soft baby pink tones, and pure white florals, every arrangement is curated with devotion to authenticity, family traditions, and contemporary grandeur.
