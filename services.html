Designed Around Your Celebration

01 — Sacred Ceremonies

Nikah Décor

The Nikah is the sanctified heart of an Islamic wedding. We design tranquil, reverent environments featuring custom seating for the bride and groom, floral enclosures, and peaceful pastel drapery that allow your sacred vows to resonate with grace.

  • Low velvet or ornate royal seating setups with ceremonial pillows
  • Hand-tied floral canopies and floral Quran stands
  • Private partitioned family viewing alcoves with sheer textures

02 — Architectural Feature

Custom Nikah Backdrops

Our bespoke backdrops serve as the iconic visual signature of your celebration. Integrating Islamic geometric arches, soft-pink floral reliefs, and customized couple calligraphy, each backdrop is custom-engineered for exceptional photography.

  • Laser-cut Moroccan and Mughal arches with warm indirect LED halos
  • Lush vertical walls of blush English roses, hydrangeas, and orchids
  • Custom acrylic or gold-leaf calligraphy with couple names and dates

03 — Botanical Elegance

Floral Arrangements

Florals are not just ornaments—they breathe life and perfume into your venue. We source fresh, premium blooms in shades of baby pink, creamy ivory, and blush rose, crafting cascading urns, banquet runners, and elevated centerpieces.

  • Cascading bridal table runners and grand urn displays
  • Aisle petals in delicate baby-pink gradients
  • Seasonal exotic blooms selected for fragrance and longevity

Stage Decoration

05 — Mood & Atmosphere

Lighting Design

Lighting creates emotion. We avoid glaring theatrical floodlights in favor of soft, diffused blush uplighting, twinkling fairy light canopies, and hundreds of real candle flames that envelop the room in romance.

  • Suspended starry fairy light ceilings and curtain cascades
  • Warm tone uplighting tailored to wedding cinematography
  • Clusters of glass hurricane lanterns with floating candles
